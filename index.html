When Flip & Invert is On, the page is pre-distorted with a preset color correction. To restore normal colors, set this on your Chromebook: Settings → Accessibility → Display and magnification → Color enhancement → Color correction: On, choose Blue-yellow (Tritanomaly), and set the intensity to 100. Also turn on Reduce animations (Settings → Accessibility → Display and magnification) to stop the page text from constantly scrambling. Turn both off again when Flip & Invert is Off.
